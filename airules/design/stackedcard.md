Each card should:

* Take 100% of the available width.
* Have **no vertical gap** between cards.
* Be visually connected as one grouped list.
* Have a subtle divider/border between each card.
* Avoid doubled borders.

Corner behavior:

* First card: rounded **top-left** and **top-right** corners only.
* Middle cards: **no border radius**.
* Last card: rounded **bottom-left** and **bottom-right** corners only.
* If there is only one card, round all four corners.

The result should look like one continuous grouped container made of multiple stacked cards, not separate floating cards.
